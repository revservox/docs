# To Optimism

For users currently on mainnet that want to migrate their position, here is a step-by-step tutorial:

1. Withdraw liquidity from your position.
   1. Connect your wallet to Revert\
      <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (79).png" alt="" data-size="original">
   2.  Locate the position you want to migrate<br>

       <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (85).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
   3.  Select 'Withdraw', set an amount and then click on 'Withdraw' button  <br>

       <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (64).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2.  Bridge your assets.

    1. Go to the [Official Optimism bridge site.](https://app.optimism.io/bridge/deposit)
    2. Bridge the assets removed from your position into Optimism

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (39).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    &#x20;3\. Wait for the bridging to confirm and once your assets are accessible on Optimism continue with the steps below.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (72).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
3.  Add liquidity to a position on Optimism



    1. Head to the revert [Add Liquidity](https://revert.finance/#/initiator) section.
    2.  Select “Optimism” as the network and define the asset pair of your position.<br>

        <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (44).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
    3.  Select the range for your new position.<br>

        <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (86).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
    4.  Add your bridged assets as liquidity for this new position on Optimism.<br>

        <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (88).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>


4. Start Auto-Compounding
   1.  Go to your new position and select “Compound fees”

       <figure><img src="https://revert.finance/assets/opti_tutorial_09.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
   2.  Click the “Activate Autocompounding” button<br>

       <figure><img src="https://revert.finance/assets/opti_tutorial_10.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
   3. Your wallet permissions will be prompted, asking for authorization for your position to be transferred to the auto-compounder contract
   4. Approve and sign the transaction
   5.  Your position will now auto-compound at optimal times given your accrued fees and gas costs. [Read more](../../auto-compounder/)<br>

       <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (62).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
   6. Rewards will be claimable from this website at the end of each incentives period.
