# To Polygon

For users currently on mainnet that want to migrate their position, here is a step-by-step tutorial:

1.  Withdraw liquidity from your position.

    1. Connect your wallet to Revert\
       <img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (79).png" alt="" data-size="original">
    2.  Locate the position you want to migrate<br>

        <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (48).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
    3. Select 'Withdraw', set an amount and then click on 'Withdraw' button &#x20;



    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (100).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
2.  Bridge your assets.

    1. Go to the [Official Polygon bridge site](https://wallet.polygon.technology/bridge/).
    2. Bridge the assets removed from your position into Optimism

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (76).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    &#x20;3\. Wait for the bridging to confirm and once your assets are accessible on Polygon continue with the steps below.<br>
3.  Add liquidity to a position on Optimism

    1. Head to the revert [Add Liquidity](https://revert.finance/#/initiator) section.
    2. Select “Polygon” as the network and define the asset pair of your position.<br>

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (99).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    3\. Select the range for your new position.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (98).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1. Add your bridged assets as liquidity for this new position on Polygon.

<figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (80).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

1.  Start Auto-Compounding

    1. Go to your new position and select “Compound fees”

    <figure><img src="../../.gitbook/assets/image (59).png" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>

    1.  Click the “Activate Autocompounding” button<br>

        <figure><img src="https://revert.finance/assets/opti_tutorial_10.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
    2. Your wallet permissions will be prompted, asking for authorization for your position to be transferred to the auto-compounder contract
    3. Approve and sign the transaction
    4.  Your position will now auto-compound at optimal times given your accrued fees and gas costs. [Read more](../../auto-compounder/)<br>

        <figure><img src="https://revert.finance/assets/opti_tutorial_11.jpg" alt=""><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
    5. Rewards will be claimable from this website at the end of each incentives period.
